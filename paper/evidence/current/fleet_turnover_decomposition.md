# Fleet Turnover And Endpoint Composition

All-record installed-capacity prevalence increases by 19.50 percentage points between FY2005 and FY2024. Among stable administrative lineages observed at both endpoints, the increase is 2.19 points; among endpoint-common lineages retaining the same reported asset episode, it is 0.88 points. The much larger repeated-cross-section change is therefore associated mainly with changing fleet-record composition rather than a widespread within-lineage state change. Because administrative appearance and disappearance are not verified openings or closures, this is a composition diagnostic rather than a physical turnover accounting identity.
