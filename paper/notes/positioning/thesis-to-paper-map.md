# Thesis-To-Paper Conversion Map

## Purpose

The thesis remains the degree-completion document. The paper is a separate,
focused analysis designed first to help a professor understand the empirical
foundation, comparator lineage, and defensible contribution. Journal compression
comes only after that foundation is accepted.

## Reframing

| Thesis-style element | Current paper treatment | Reason |
|:--|:--|:--|
| Broad concern about unused incineration energy | Begin with the FY2024 count-volume divergence: 41.1% facility participation versus 80.1% throughput coverage | Replaces a potentially misleading count-only problem statement |
| Survey facility code as the panel key | Use 1,690 reconstructed stable administrative lineages and 1,767 asset episodes from 23,593 retained records, exposing 16 uncertain links | Makes the longitudinal assumption explicit and auditable |
| One transition story | Separate fleet coverage, first reported installed-capacity entry, and generator components | Prevents unlike constructs from being combined |
| Conventional entry regression | Use Firth bias-reduced discrete-time models for 35 broad, 33 prior-operation, and 24 same-episode events plus identity-certain sensitivity | Matches the sparse-event design and continuity uncertainty |
| Age-centered entry narrative | Treat scale as the stable result; report continuity-sensitive joint age tests rather than a universal headline | Aligns prose with formal inference |
| Gross MWh/t as performance or efficiency | Decompose generator design intensity, electrical capacity factor, and waste loading | Restores engineering construct validity |
| Policy prescription | Offer diagnostic implications and explicit evidence boundaries | The design is observational and does not rank investments |

## Paper Questions

1. How different are facility participation, waste-throughput coverage, and
   waste-processing design-capacity coverage?
2. Among reconstructed sites first observed without installed generation, which
   prior characteristics are associated with first reported positive capacity?
3. Among engineering-valid operating generators, how do installed generator
   sizing and annual electrical capacity factor structure gross generation per
   tonne?

These questions form one progression: **how much of the fleet participates,
which sites enter, and what produces observed output among participants**.

## What To Retain From The Thesis

- national administrative scope and transparent variable construction
- careful institutional explanation for readers unfamiliar with Japanese waste
  incineration
- reproducible evidence chain from raw workbooks to manuscript claims
- explicit limitations and noncausal language
- enough methodological detail for a professor to reconstruct each estimand

## What To Remove Or Relocate

- thesis-chapter signposting and repeated motivation
- broad climate or governance claims not measured in the analysis
- long estimator catalogs that do not change the selected estimand
- any model of administrative record disappearance
- any interpretation of survey codes as persistent facilities
- any age or utilization claim based on a gross-output model that omits installed
  generator sizing
- generic planning tables that outrun the evidence

Detailed linkage rules, robustness variants, and pathway classifications belong
in the supplement, but their purpose and main consequences must remain clear in
the main text.

## Comparator Adaptation

Use the [professor comparator and method lineage](professor-comparator-method-lineage.md)
to explain intellectual provenance.

| What is learned from comparators | How this paper adapts it | What is not copied or claimed |
|:--|:--|:--|
| National plant-level framing | Extends the frame to the pre-generation state and audited site lineages | No claim of an identical sample, outcome, or national policy design |
| Explicit engineering structure | Separates installed sizing from annual use with an accounting identity | No thermodynamic, lifecycle, or optimization model |
| Transparent estimator-to-question alignment | Uses Firth inference because first entries are sparse | No claim that bias reduction creates causal identification |
| High-profile visual and narrative discipline | Uses one argument per figure and an answer-first results sequence | No claim of comparator-level technical scope or scenario modeling |

The paper is original when it states the borrowed principle, documents the
adaptation, and shows that the resulting estimands and evidence are different.

## Revision Order

1. Freeze generated evidence and pass claim verification.
2. Align abstract, research questions, equations, tables, and conclusions to the
   same constructs.
3. Ensure the professor-facing draft explains linkage, Firth inference, and the
   engineering identity without requiring the supplement.
4. Ask for feedback on the question and estimands before adding new analysis.
5. Compress for a target journal only after the empirical foundation is stable.

## Completion Test

A professor unfamiliar with the repository should be able to explain, after one
read, why the 41.1% count is incomplete, why 35 events require Firth inference,
why age is not the entry headline, and why installed generator sizing changes
the interpretation of gross MWh/t.
