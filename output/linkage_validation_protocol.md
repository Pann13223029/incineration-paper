# Blinded Linkage-Validation Protocol

The review packet hides algorithmic scores, match methods, and final lineage labels. Reviewers compare the prior and current administrative records and classify each pair before opening the answer key.

## Packet Coverage

- Packet Pairs: 558
- Modeled Event Links: 35
- Uncertain Links: 16
- Fuzzy Links: 375
- Gap Links: 31
- Fy2019 2020 Bridges: 50

## Allowed Decisions

- `same administrative facility history`
- `different facility history`
- `indeterminate from available evidence`
- `same lineage but probable asset/configuration reset`

A second reviewer should independently assess every modeled-event link, every uncertain link, and a blinded subset of the remaining packet. Disagreements require adjudication with an archived municipal or Ministry source. Completed decisions belong in a dated review copy, not in the generated packet.
