# Fleet Coverage And Conditional Generation Intensity

The fleet result is decomposed into the share of recorded waste throughput handled by facilities with plausible positive generation and the weighted gross generation intensity within those facilities. This prevents a facility count from being interpreted as a waste-volume or energy-output share.

## FY2024 Headline

- Installed-generation facility participation: 41.1% of all records; 46.4% of positive-throughput records
- Throughput handled by positive-output facilities: 80.1%
- Positive-output facility participation: 40.4% of all records; 46.6% of positive-throughput records
- Waste-processing design capacity at installed-generation facilities: 70.5%
- Engineering-valid throughput coverage: 79.7%
- Conditional engineering-valid gross intensity: 0.425 MWh/t
- Engineering-valid fleet gross intensity: 0.338 MWh per total tonne

The exact identity is:

`fleet gross MWh / total tonnes = valid generator-throughput share x conditional valid generator MWh/t`.
